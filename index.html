Joshua Kratt First Round Mock Drafts.

Every first-round mock I made over five drafts, checked pick-by-pick against the real results. This is how it went.

These mocks were never meant to be predictions. For each pick, I drafted as if I were the GM of that team, the player I'd take in their shoes, not the player I expected them to take.

So the grading below works two ways: whether I called the actual pick, and separately, whether my pick would have actually been the better call.
